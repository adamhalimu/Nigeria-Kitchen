How to Make Homemade Nigerian Shawarma

Discover the ingredients and preparation techniques behind one of Nigeria's popular street-food favourites.

Nigerian Kitchen Journal Cooking Guide

Shawarma is a popular wrap enjoyed in Nigeria. It typically combines seasoned meat, vegetables and sauces inside flatbread, creating a convenient and satisfying meal.

What Is Nigerian Shawarma?

Nigerian-style shawarma is influenced by the traditional Middle Eastern wrap but often features locally preferred ingredients, seasonings and sauces.

Common fillings include chicken or beef, cabbage, carrots, onions and creamy sauces. Recipes vary according to individual taste.

Ingredients

Here is a suggested list of ingredients for a homemade version. Confirm the exact ingredients and quantities with Nigerian Kitchen before publishing this as its official recipe.

For the Filling

  • Chicken or beef strips
  • Shredded cabbage
  • Grated carrots
  • Sliced onions
  • Seasoning and spices
  • Cooking oil

For the Wrap

  • Shawarma bread or flatbread
  • Mayonnaise
  • Ketchup or another preferred sauce
  • Other sauces of choice

How to Prepare Homemade Nigerian Shawarma

Step 1: Prepare the Meat

Cut your preferred meat into suitable strips. Season with your chosen spices and allow the flavours to develop before cooking.

Step 2: Cook the Meat

Cook the seasoned meat thoroughly using your preferred cooking method. Ensure chicken or other meat is safely cooked before assembling the shawarma.

Step 3: Prepare the Vegetables

Wash and slice the cabbage, carrots and onions. Keep the vegetables ready for assembling.

Step 4: Prepare the Sauce

Combine your preferred sauces in a small bowl. Adjust the mixture according to your taste.

Step 5: Assemble the Shawarma

Place the flatbread on a clean surface. Add the cooked meat, vegetables and sauce to the centre of the bread.

Step 6: Wrap and Heat

Fold the bread around the filling and heat the assembled shawarma using a suitable grill or pan until warmed and lightly toasted.

Tips for Making Delicious Shawarma

  • Use fresh vegetables for better texture.
  • Season the meat properly before cooking.
  • Avoid overfilling the flatbread.
  • Balance the sauces to suit your preference.
  • Wrap the bread firmly to keep the filling inside.
  • Serve while warm for the best experience.

Enjoying Nigerian Shawarma

Shawarma can be enjoyed as a quick lunch, evening snack or convenient meal. Its combination of seasoned meat, fresh vegetables and sauces makes it a popular choice for many food lovers.
