How to Prepare Nigerian Catfish Pepper Soup

Discover the ingredients and cooking techniques behind this flavourful Nigerian delicacy.

Nigerian Kitchen Journal Recipe Guide

Catfish Pepper Soup is a popular Nigerian dish recognised for its spicy broth, distinctive seasoning and tender fish. It is enjoyed in different parts of the country and can be served as a meal or appetiser.

What Is Catfish Pepper Soup?

Catfish Pepper Soup is prepared by cooking catfish in a seasoned broth with pepper and aromatic spices. Its distinctive flavour comes from the combination of fish, traditional spices and other ingredients.

Preparation methods and spice combinations can vary depending on personal preference and regional cooking traditions.

Ingredients

The following is a suggested ingredient list. Confirm the restaurant's actual recipe and quantities before publishing.

  • Fresh catfish
  • Scotch bonnet pepper
  • Onions
  • Ground crayfish, if desired
  • Traditional pepper soup spice blend
  • Seasoning
  • Salt
  • Fresh scent leaves or preferred herbs
  • Water

How to Prepare Catfish Pepper Soup

Step 1: Clean the Catfish

Clean the catfish carefully and cut it into suitable portions. Handle the fish gently to keep the pieces intact.

Step 2: Prepare the Ingredients

Wash and prepare the onions, peppers and herbs. Keep your pepper soup spices ready before cooking.

Step 3: Prepare the Broth

Add water to a suitable pot. Introduce the onions, pepper soup spices and other selected seasonings to prepare the broth.

Step 4: Add the Catfish

Carefully add the cleaned catfish portions to the broth. Avoid excessive stirring so the fish pieces remain intact.

Step 5: Cook the Soup

Allow the fish to cook gently until thoroughly cooked. Check the seasoning and adjust to your preferred taste.

Step 6: Add the Herbs and Serve

Add your preferred fresh herbs towards the end of cooking. Serve the pepper soup hot.

How Is Catfish Pepper Soup Served?

Catfish Pepper Soup can be enjoyed on its own or alongside a preferred accompaniment. Serving styles vary from one household and restaurant to another.
